# Future Product Pillars

Once the technical foundation is stable, future-facing WYD evolution should focus on:

1. live-operable content and seasons;
2. modern account progression and collections;
3. strong PvP/guild competition with transparent ranking;
4. instanced cooperative content and world events;
5. modern launcher, patching and account experience;
6. GM/admin tooling with auditability;
7. economy telemetry and anti-abuse controls;
8. performance and reliability suitable for larger concurrent populations.

Specific features remain product decisions and should not be hardwired into infrastructure refactors.
